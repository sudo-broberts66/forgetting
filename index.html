Limits of this game

The shifts are simplified, informal versions of laboratory tasks. They use café orders and names where the original studies used letters, digits and word lists, each runs only a few trials, and the results cannot measure your memory or compare it with anyone else's. Where a number comes from a study, it is the figure reported there or, where marked approximate, the figure as commonly reported from the study's graphs.
